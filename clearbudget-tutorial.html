📘 ClearBudget – Help Center

⭐ Premium Version

The free version of ClearBudget has a limited number of clients and transactions. You can keep using it by watching ads or earning points. The Premium version gives you:
• Unlimited clients, transactions, and invoices – for life
• All ads removed
• Future exclusive features
• Priority support Payment methods:
• Through Google Play (if you have a Google account and a payment card)
• Or by contacting the developer to pay via: Whish, OMT, cryptocurrencies, and more After direct payment, you receive an activation code to enter inside the app.

👤 Personal Information Page

This page allows you to personalize your reports and invoices with your name, signature, and company logo. You may edit any information at any time and press (Save). All your data stays on your device only and is never sent anywhere. You may also leave the fields empty if you do not wish to display any identity on your invoices.

💱 Currency Settings

Select the currency you want to work with inside the app (USD – EUR – GBP – LBP – or your local currency). When you change the currency, the app automatically converts all previous transactions to the new currency. You may switch anytime, and reports are updated instantly.

☁️ Backup & Restore

ClearBudget provides two backup methods: 1) Google Drive Backup: • Requires Google Login • Daily automatic backup • Manual (instant) backup • Restore the latest cloud backup with one tap 2) Manual Backup (ZIP file): • Export your data anywhere: phone, WhatsApp, email, or any cloud storage • Restore any file you choose ⚠️ Note: restoring will delete your current data and replace it with the selected backup.

👥 Clients Page

Tap (+) to add a new client. You may add a name and phone number — the number is optional but useful for calling or sending messages. Long-press on a client to edit, delete, or change their type among:
• General – General (Local)
• Client (USD) – Client (Local)
• Supplier (USD) – Supplier (Local) Each client’s balance is calculated automatically from their transactions.

💼 Transactions Page

Record any operation here: payment, receipt, debt, purchase, or sale. Extra features:
• PDF button to generate a ready account statement for WhatsApp sharing
• Invoice button to create an invoice or payment receipt
• All generated documents are automatically saved in the Documents Archive page

📂 Categories

View detailed statistics by type of client: Clients – Suppliers – General – Local – USD. You can see: • Amounts you are owed • Amounts you owe • Based on the selected currency (USD – Local)

📊 Reports

A powerful page for analyzing your business: • Top debtors • Best clients • Aging of debts (old – recent) • Cash flow for every month You may easily switch currencies and browse monthly data instantly.

🧾 Documents & Invoices

This page contains all documents you created: • Invoices • Payment receipts • PDF account statements You can reopen any document, share it, or delete it.

⏰ Reminders

Create reminders linked to any client: • Payment reminders • Follow-up notes • Due dates • Client-specific comments You can call or send WhatsApp messages directly from the reminder card.

🔒 Security

Protect your data by enabling PIN lock or fingerprint. Extra options:
• Change your PIN
• Regain access via fingerprint or smart recovery
• Emergency recovery code
