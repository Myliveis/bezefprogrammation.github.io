⭐ Premium Version

The free version has limits for clients and transactions. Premium unlocks lifetime access with no ads. You can upgrade through Google Play, or contact the developer to get a Premium activation code.

👤 Personal Information

Add your name, phone, and business details to show them on invoices and statements. You may leave the fields empty if you prefer not to personalize documents.

💱 Currency Settings

Choose your main local currency. When switching the currency, all “Local” transactions will update automatically.

☁ Backup & Restore

ClearBudget supports:
• Automatic daily backup to Google Drive
• Manual Drive backup
• Manual file export/import ⚠ Restoring a backup will delete current data and replace it.

👥 Clients Page

Press (+) to add a new client. Long-press a client to edit, delete, or switch between 6 roles (General / Dollar / Local / Customer / Supplier / etc.).

💵 Transactions

Add payments, receipts, reminders, or notes. Generate a PDF statement instantly or create invoices & receipts. All generated documents are saved in the Documents Archive.

📊 Reports

View Top Debtors, Cash Flow, Best Clients, and Debt Aging charts. Switch between USD and Local currency anytime.

🏷 Categories

See grouped client statistics with totals for each category. Tap any category to see all clients and their balances.

🔔 Reminders

Create reminders to follow up with clients. You can call or WhatsApp them directly from the reminder card.
